Which component approach do you prefer?

A

Separate SummaryCard & DetailsCard components Recommended

Replace the generic MetricCard with two purpose-built components matching Angular's app-sqf-summary-card and app-sqf-details-card exactly. Cleaner separation, exact feature parity.
